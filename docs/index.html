Indus Asset Viewer — Phase 7: Battle Royale Buildings with Interior Rooms + Golden Sandstone Mahal

New — interior rooms: the 11 buildings are no longer one empty shell. Each floor is now divided by 0.10 m interior partition walls — 2×2 blocks get a 15 m wall down the middle plus a jog wall (living room, hallway, bedroom), 3×2 blocks get a 5 m central hallway with three rooms either side, 1×1 huts get a front room + bedroom. Every partition has a 1.5 m doorway, and the build refuses to finish if a partition blocks a doorway, cuts the staircase, or leaves a room unreachable. Furniture is assigned per room: beds in the bedroom, tables/shelves/counters in the living room & kitchen, crates scattered through every room so loot has to be searched for.
New palette: every Mahal wall is now golden sandstone #D9C089 (Amber Fort / Jaisalmer) with cream #EDDBB3 highlights and tan #B39466 carving shadows — gold trim, teal domes and deep-blue doors/windows kept exactly as they were (the 50×50 Hot Drop fortress now uses the same golden sandstone map). The Rajasthani Mahal — Room-by-Room Modular Assembly: four walk-in rooms (Darbar Hall 20×15 m with dais, golden throne & 8 banded pillars, Courtyard Aangan 20×20 m open to the sky with a marble fountain at the exact centre & arched jharokha walkways, Ranivas 15×10 m with two rooms, a corridor, protruding cube patterns & balconies, and the Gateway 15×5 m with a multi-layered scalloped Mehrab and open wooden doors) assembled into one gap-free 50×40 m, 23.8 m tall palace — courtyard at (0,0,0), darbar north at Y=17.5, ranivas at X=±17.5, gateway south at Y=−12.5, a second floor of the same rooms scaled 0.85, flat roof over everything except the aangan, a large central teal dome and 4 golden corner domes. Plus the rest of the world — low-poly Battle Royale map environment built with Blender bpy for 4GB RAM devices: featuring the Mahal Palace (50m×50m × 15m, walls 15m thick) Hot Drop Fortress, Vibrant Stylized Palace (40m×30m base, 30m×20m×15m walls), Rajasthani Mahal Master — Exterior + Royal Darbar Interior (40m×30m base, 30m×20m×12m walls, sawtooth parapet, central teal dome r6 + 4 yellow r3, chhatris, checkered floor, 8 pillars, throne, jali, chandelier) and Modular Palace Blocks (wall 10×0.5×4 + gold border, arch 10×0.5×6 with r2 cutter + torus frame, dome r5 + spire, pillar r0.5 h6 with 3 bands) — hollow Boolean bases, trig arches via math.sin/cos, sliced domes with Kalash/cylinder+cone spires, plus Terrain (1000m ground, mountains, plateaus, valleys), Water & Bridges (ocean, river, beach, dam, bridges), Nature (trees, rocks, bushes), and Phase 1 Buildings. All props use 1K/2K PBR textures & solid Principled BSDF with bottom-center (0,0,0) origins.
