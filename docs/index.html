Indus Asset Viewer — Mount Abu · Elite Cargo Dropship · The Grand Mahal · Modular Buildings · Terrain

A focused gallery of the production-ready bpy asset sets: the Mount Abu hill station (150 m mesa, 78° cliffs, a drivable corniche and Nakki Lake), the Elite Cargo Dropship that opens the drop sequence (45 m span, 4 nacelles, open rear ramp), the Grand Mahal (golden sandstone, 50 × 40 m, 23.8 m tall, with a walk-in darbar, courtyard, ranivas wings and gateway), the 11 Modular Buildings (15 m grid, real interior rooms, per-building palettes and furniture) and the terrain tiles (Mount Abu hill station plus the 1000 m ground, hill, plateau and valley). Every asset is generated by bpy in this repo, joined into one flat-shaded mesh, uses solid Principled materials with a bottom-centre (0,0,0) origin, and is regenerated and redeployed by GitHub Actions on every push to main.

Generated by bpy · tools/convert_character_obj.py (Player Tactical — OBJ → solid-colour GLB) · tools/generate_mount_abu.py (Mount Abu, 150 m mesa + corniche + lake) · tools/generate_plane_cargo_elite.py (Elite Cargo Dropship, 45 m span) · tools/generate_mahal_rooms.py (4 walk-in rooms → rajasthani_mahal_mega.glb, 50 × 40 m) · tools/generate_building_modules.py + tools/assemble_buildings.py (15 m kit → 11 buildings) · tools/generate_terrain.py (ground, hill, plateau, valley) → exported as packed .glb, deployed to GitHub Pages by GitHub Actions on every push to main. Only the production asset sets are kept in this repository.
