## Origins
Every room's origin is its **bottom-centre at the floor** — `(0, 0, 0)` sits on the ground in the middle of the room, so
instancing a room at `Vector3.ZERO` in Godot/Unity puts its floor exactly on your terrain (no Y offset). Decorative
overhangs (ranivas balconies +1.45 m, gate Mehrab trim +0.28 m, darbar arch trim +0.07 m) extend past the nominal
footprint and are included in the measured bbox.

The **mega assembly keeps the courtyard at the origin**: `(0, 0, 0)` = courtyard centre, ground level. The bbox therefore
spans X −27…27, Y −20.5…20 and Z 0…23.78. North is Blender **+Y** (glTF **−Z**).

## Godot usage
```gdscript
# single mega palace
var mega := preload("res://assets/downloaded_models/mahal/rajasthani_mahal_mega.glb").instantiate()
mega.position = Vector3.ZERO          # courtyard centre, floor on the ground
add_child(mega)

# or build from rooms (no scaling on the ground floor!)
for room in ["room_darbar_hall", "room_courtyard", "room_ranivas", "room_gateway"]:
    var s := load("res://assets/downloaded_models/mahal/rooms/%s.glb" % room).instantiate()
    add_child(s)
```
Generate collision with `MeshInstance3D.create_trimesh_shape()` (ConcavePolygonShape3D) — see
`examples/godot/main_mahal_godot.gd` for a ready-made recursive loader.
